Operations Desk / Hrvatski

AI quote automation, with your rules and your final decision.

AI Operations Desk demonstrates sales request automation for an office furniture business. Choose a product, quantity, discount and deadline. The workflow checks a synthetic catalog and prepares a draft quote.

How the demo works

  1. Check stock and lead times against a local catalog.
  2. Calculate prices and discounts in code.
  3. Prepare an AI brief with identified source records.
  4. Pause execution for human approval when the discount exceeds 8%.
  5. Prepare a customer reply draft and export a text quote.

What can you try?

Three prepared scenarios: a discount decision, a stock shortage with an available alternative, and a quote that meets the demo policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does this demo place an order or send an email?

No. It prepares drafts, sends no messages and reserves no stock.

Are the prices and stock real?

No. Business records are synthetic examples. Totals are calculated in code, excluding VAT.

Where does AI fit?

Gemini and OpenAI modes use the selected model to compose a brief grounded in checked data. Demo mode makes no model calls. The interface renders typed OpenUI components.
