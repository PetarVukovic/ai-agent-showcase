AI RFP analysis with a proposal you can review.

AI Proposal Studio is a working MVP that extracts selected requirements from a project brief, compares them with a defined service catalogue and prepares a proposal draft. Every displayed source quote is verified against the actual page text.

Try the demo

How does the AI proposal generator work?

  1. Choose one of two synthetic scenarios or upload a text PDF / TXT file.
  2. Run Gemini or OpenAI analysis, or the clearly labelled rule-based guided demo.
  3. Review selected requirements, catalogue coverage and exact source passages.
  4. Include or exclude services and update the draft without another model call.
  5. Review clarification questions and download the proposal in Markdown format.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a full compliance review?

No. AI analysis extracts up to 12 selected requirements. Review the full document and every conclusion.

Are the prices a real commercial offer?

No. Amounts are calculated from a synthetic sample price list, without VAT. They are not a commercial offer from Petar Vuković.

Does it support scanned documents?

The MVP uses PyMuPDF to extract existing PDF text. It does not run OCR. Use a text PDF or UTF-8 TXT file.

Where are documents processed?

The application runs locally. Gemini and OpenAI modes send document text to the selected model provider. Guided demo mode makes no model call.

Does it send the proposal to the customer?

No. Export creates a draft for human review. Nothing is sent to a customer.

What does this demo demonstrate?

AI RFP response assistance, service catalogue matching, evidence verification and AI proposal generation with citations and human review. Built with Python, LangGraph, React, TypeScript, OpenUI and SQLite.
